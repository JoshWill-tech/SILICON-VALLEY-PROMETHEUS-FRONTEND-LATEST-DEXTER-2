Expert procedural guidance for mapping codebase structure and dependencies.

## Instructions
- Use `glob` and `list_directory` to build a mental model of the project structure.
- Trace imports and exports to understand component relationships.
- Identify circular dependencies and high-coupling areas.
- Document architectural patterns (e.g., MVC, Layered, Clean Architecture).
- Use `grep_search` to find usages of specific symbols across the repository.
