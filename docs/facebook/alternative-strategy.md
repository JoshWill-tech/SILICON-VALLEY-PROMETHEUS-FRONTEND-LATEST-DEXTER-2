# Facebook and Instagram Alternative Strategy
Date: 2026-06-08

## Current State
Facebook access is blocked because the account is banned or unavailable. Instagram Graph API access is tied to Meta business account health, so Instagram publishing should also be treated as blocked until Meta recovery succeeds.

## Recommended Product Handling
- Show Facebook as blocked: "Unavailable - account banned."
- Show Instagram as blocked: "Unavailable - Facebook account banned."
- Disable publishing actions for blocked Meta platforms.
- Do not display fake connected badges, fake captions, or fake post URLs.
- Keep a public waitlist or "Notify me" action if needed.

## Recovery Options
1. Appeal the ban with the business-focused template in this folder.
2. Create a new Meta business account only if allowed under Meta rules and backed by business verification.
3. Defer Facebook/Instagram and focus on YouTube, TikTok, Dropbox, LinkedIn, and X first.

## Why Defer Is Reasonable
Meta review can be slow and account-health dependent. A blocked integration should not delay operational platforms. Honest staging reduces user trust damage and avoids platform-policy misrepresentation.
