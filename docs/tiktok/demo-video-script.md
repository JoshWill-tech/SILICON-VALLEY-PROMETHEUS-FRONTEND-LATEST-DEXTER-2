# TikTok Demo Video Script
Date: 2026-06-08

## Target Length
2 to 4 minutes.

## Script
1. Open `https://prometheusstudio.tech`.
2. Show visible Privacy Policy and Terms links without opening a menu.
3. Sign in with the TikTok review demo account.
4. Open Settings, Social Accounts.
5. Click Connect TikTok.
6. Complete TikTok OAuth consent.
7. Show TikTok status as connected.
8. Open an existing video project.
9. Show the completed export.
10. Click Publish or Social Publishing.
11. Select TikTok.
12. Review caption and video details.
13. Click Publish.
14. Show success state or staging state if production posting is awaiting final approval.
15. Return to Settings, Social Accounts.
16. Disconnect TikTok.

## Narration
The TikTok integration is optional. Prometheus Studio publishes only videos selected by the user. We do not post automatically, access private messages, or share TikTok data for advertising.
