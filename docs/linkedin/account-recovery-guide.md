# LinkedIn Account Recovery Guide
Date: 2026-06-08

## Situation
The LinkedIn developer account is disabled or unavailable. The priority is to recover the business account with a professional, policy-aligned explanation.

## Recovery Steps
1. Contact LinkedIn Developer Support from a business email.
2. Provide business identity and domain: `prometheusstudio.tech`.
3. Provide privacy policy and terms URLs.
4. Explain the professional use case: user-initiated publishing of completed video content by businesses and creators.
5. State that Prometheus Studio does not scrape LinkedIn, does not automate unsolicited posting, and does not access data beyond approved API scopes.
6. Ask for the reason for disablement and required remediation steps.

## Business Verification Packet
- Business name: Prometheus AI / Prometheus Studio.
- Support email: support@prometheusstudio.tech.
- Website: `https://prometheusstudio.tech`.
- Privacy: `https://prometheusstudio.tech/privacy`.
- Terms: `https://prometheusstudio.tech/terms`.
- Product summary: AI-assisted video editing and user-directed social publishing.

## If Recovery Fails
Create a new developer account with a business-controlled email and verified company profile. Do not reuse personal-only identity details or outdated app descriptions.
